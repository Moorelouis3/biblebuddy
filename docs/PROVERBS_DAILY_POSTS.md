# The Wisdom of Proverbs: 31 Daily Group Posts

For Louis to review. These are the posts the daily cron
(`/api/cron/community-event-daily`, 10:00 UTC = 6:00 a.m. US Eastern) publishes
as Louis in the Bible Buddy group, Oct 1 to Oct 31 2026, one per day.

- Source of the words: `lib/proverbsDailyPosts.ts` (edit there, then regenerate this file).
- Each summary and question is based on that day's devotional in the app.
- Key verses are copied from the app's KJV text (`public/kjv/proverbs/*.json`) and checked word for word.
- Every post links to that day's study, and its replies are the day's discussion.
- Notifications: Day 1 goes to every member of the event. Days 2 to 31 go only to members who turned reminders on.
  They read: "Louis Moore posted Day N of The Wisdom of Proverbs: <title>".

## Day 1: Whose Voice Will You Listen To?

> Day 1 of 31 · Proverbs 1  
>
> There are a lot of voices trying to tell you how to live, and Proverbs 1 opens by asking which one you will follow. Solomon, the king who asked God for wisdom, lays the foundation for the whole book: wisdom starts with God, not with you. Then another voice shows up saying 'come with us,' and the chapter shows exactly where that road ends.
