Settings
Settings becomes short grouped rows like an iPhone settings screen. Each row opens its own page, so the long scroll of big cards goes away. Log out, account details and delete sit in an Account group below.
Bible covers are the old skins, shown as a shelf of Bibles. Classic is today's plain look. The photos are the ones still on disk, already compressed.
On the home screen the cover sits behind the cards with a slow drift (and gentle snow on Winter Cabin). It stops for anyone with Reduce Motion on.
