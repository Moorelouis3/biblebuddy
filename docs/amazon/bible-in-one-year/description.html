Most people never read the whole Bible. Not because they do not want to.

They start in Genesis with good intentions, get lost somewhere in the laws of Leviticus, and quietly stop. A year later the bookmark has not moved.

Bible in One Year is written so that does not happen to you.

It takes you through the entire Bible in 365 days. Genesis to Revelation. Every book, every chapter, one day at a time.

Here is how a day works.

  • You are given the day's reading, and you read those chapters in your own Bible.
  • You turn to the page for that day, and it walks you through what you just read.
  • What happened. Why it matters. What it means for your life today.

Every book of the Bible opens with its own introduction, telling you who wrote it, what it covers, and what to watch for before you begin. Scripture is quoted throughout from the King James Version.

But this book is not here to help you finish the Bible. It is here to help you see it.

The Bible is not sixty-six separate books stacked on a shelf. It is one story. A garden at the beginning. A city at the end. And in between, a God who keeps coming after people who keep running from Him.

So one question follows you through the whole year: where is God in this story? Not just what happened, and not just who did what. Where is God, and what is He doing? Some days the answer is right on the surface. Some days He is quiet and you have to look for Him. But He is in every book, even in Esther, where His name never appears once.

365 days, but not a race.

Some days are three chapters. Some days are one long psalm. If you miss a day, do not quit, and do not try to catch up by reading five at once. Just pick up where you left off. The plan will wait for you.

The goal is not to finish fast. The goal is to finish.

Who this is for

  • Anyone who has started the Bible more than once and never got to the end
  • New believers who want somewhere to begin and something to hold on to
  • Anyone who knows the famous stories but has never seen how they connect
  • Small groups, couples and families reading through the year together

And when you reach Day 365, do not put it on a shelf. Start again. You will not be the same person who opened it the first time, and the same chapters will say things to you that you did not hear the year before.
