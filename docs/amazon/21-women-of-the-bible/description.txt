Twenty one women. Twenty one days. One God who has never worked only through the people the world noticed.

From Eve in the garden to Martha and Mary in the house at Bethany, the women of Scripture were not on the margins of God's story. They carried it.

21 Women of the Bible: A 21-Day Study of the Remarkable Women God Used to Shape His Story spends a day with each of them, telling her story straight and letting it land on your life.

Each day follows the same path:
• Read the passage, with what to watch for before you start.
• Walk through her story in plain English, with the Scripture on the page.
• Sit with reflection questions that are hard to dodge.
• Take one concrete action.
• Close in prayer.

These are real women, really recorded.

Some are brave. Some are broken. Some did terrible things. Jezebel is in here, and so is Delilah, and none of them have been cleaned up. Sarah laughed at God. Hagar was used and discarded, and met the God who saw her. Leah spent a lifetime being the one who was not chosen. Rahab bet everything on a God she had only heard about. Esther risked her life. Mary said yes before she knew the cost.

It does not pretend every story ends happily. Some of these women died waiting. Some were never loved the way they deserved. That is faced honestly, because God's love has never meant He prevents every hurt. It means He sees, He hears, and He keeps His promises.

If you have ever felt overlooked, this is a study about the God who looks.

Begin with Day 1 and move through the month, or take three days with one woman whose story will not let you go.
