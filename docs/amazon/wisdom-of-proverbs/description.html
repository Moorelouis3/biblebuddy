What would change if you brought the wisdom of Proverbs into your everyday decisions?

The way you speak. How you handle money. What you do when you are angry. The friendships you choose. The advice you follow.

The Wisdom of Proverbs: A 31 Day Bible Study guides you through all 31 chapters of Proverbs, one chapter each day, helping you understand Scripture and apply it to the life you are actually living.

Each daily study follows a clear path:

  • Read the chapter of Proverbs.
  • Explore its meaning and connections to the rest of Scripture.
  • Consider how its wisdom applies to your everyday life.
  • Answer reflection questions that invite honest examination.
  • Take one concrete action.
  • Respond in prayer.

Explore wisdom for the decisions that matter.

Work through themes including words, money, anger, friendships, integrity and the influences shaping your choices. Move beyond recognising a wise saying to considering what it asks of you.

Whether you are beginning to explore Proverbs or returning with fresh questions, this study offers a structured way to engage with one chapter at a time.

Begin with Day 1 and follow the study through the month, or take more time with the chapters that challenge you.

Wisdom is not merely something you know. It is something you live.
