You promised yourself this time would be different.

Maybe you said it out loud. Maybe you said it at two in the morning. Maybe you poured it down the sink, deleted the app, threw the stash away, or swore you were done for good.

Then you did it again.

Higher Hope is written for the person living inside that loop.

It is a 60 day devotional for anyone fighting something they keep going back to. Weed. Alcohol. Pills. Porn. Gambling. Food. The phone in your hand. The thing itself changes. The loop does not.

This is not a book written from a safe distance.

Louis Moore III struggled with weed for twenty five years, and smoked every day for the last five of them. He stole money to buy it. He drove hundreds of miles to get it. One day he had ten dollars to his name and chose weed over food. He tried to quit over and over and never made it past a week.

What finally changed is what this book is about.

Here is how a day works.

- A short Bible reading, with what to watch for before you start.
- A study that opens the passage up in plain language and brings it honestly into your fight.
- Reflection questions that are actually hard to answer.
- One concrete action for the day.
- A prayer, written for the days you cannot find your own words.

Sixty days, in three parts.

- Days 1 to 20, Understanding the Struggle. The loop, the lies, the triggers, and the God who meets you in the middle of it.
- Days 21 to 40, Breaking the Cycle. What to run to instead, how to keep going when it is slow, and what to do with a day you fall.
- Days 41 to 60, Learning to Live Free. A new mind, repaired relationships, and a life that is bigger than not using.

Do not let the number put pressure on you. Three days on one chapter is better than rushing three to stay on schedule.

What this book will not do.

It will not tell you that enough faith makes withdrawal painless. It will not tell you that a slip means you were never serious. And it will not tell you to skip the doctor, the counselor or the program. Day 45 is about withdrawal and Day 46 is about knowing when you need more help than a book can give.

Scripture throughout is the King James Version.

Who this is for

- Anyone who has tried to stop more times than they want to admit
- Christians who feel ashamed that this is still a fight
- People in recovery who want God in it and not just a programme
- Parents, spouses and friends who want to understand what the person they love is up against

You are not the first person to live in that loop. One of the greatest Christians who ever lived wrote about it in the Bible, in plain words, and God put it there so you would know you are not alone.

A Bible Buddy study by Louis Moore III.
