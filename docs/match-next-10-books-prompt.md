**THIS BOOK: 1 Kings**

You are making boards for **Bible Buddy Match**, a match-3 game in the Bible Buddy app. The
player walks a painted map through the Bible, one board per Bible story. Genesis through
1 Samuel are finished, and **every new book has to look like it was painted by the same artist
on the same day.** I have attached maps, backgrounds, a win screen and tiles from the finished
books. **Study them first. Everything you make must match them:** the same painterly realism,
the same warm golden light and haze, the same muted earthy colors, the same calm.

Make **only the book named at the top**, using its table below. When it is delivered I will
ask for the next one.

## Style rules (read these twice)

1. **Board backgrounds and the map are landscapes with NO people.** Nobody at
   all: no characters, no crowds, no faces, no figures in the distance. Only the places of this
   book: its hills, rivers, cities, roads and sky. A busy picture makes the board hard to play.
   (Win screens are the one exception: they tell the story, so they show its people.)
2. **The middle of each background stays calm.** The game board covers the middle 60%. Keep
   it simple: ground, mist, plain sky. Put the interesting things in the top 20% and rocks or
   ground in the bottom 15%.
3. **Same light and colors as the attached pictures:** warm, golden, hazy, cinematic, like a
   movie matte painting. **No** bright saturated blue sky, **no** glowing cartoon clouds,
   **no** toy-bright colors, **no** children's-cartoon look.
4. No text, buttons, UI or frames on any background or map.
5. Gentle and hopeful, never scary or gory. No blood, no bodies, no executions, no battle
   violence: war and judgment are shown as distant smoke, empty roads, or a city on the
   horizon.
