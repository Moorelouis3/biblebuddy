# The Play Store tester email

Ready to send. Written 2026-11-03, the day Google approved the closed test.

## Why it is shaped like this

The app is **on Android already** - it is just in closed testing, which is a
stage Google puts every new Android app through rather than a problem with
ours. The email has to say that, because "almost on the Play Store" reads
like something went wrong. It did not.

Google's bar: **12 testers with the app still installed after 14 unbroken
days.** Not 12 clicks. If someone installs it and deletes it on day three the
count drops and the clock suffers, so the email welcomes more than 12 without
making 12 sound optional. There is no upper limit worth worrying about - 12
is a floor.

Two steps, and they cannot be merged. Joining the group is what grants
permission to see the test; the second link is what installs it. Anyone who
skips step one gets what looks like a broken link, so the email says so
plainly. The group is set to "anyone on the web can join", so nobody waits
for approval and Louis collects no addresses.

Send to the whole list. Twelve out of several thousand is a low bar.

## The email

Bible Buddy is on Android.

It is not in the Play Store for everyone yet, and there is a reason for that.
Google puts every new Android app through a closed test first - real people,
on real phones, for two weeks. Only after that will they open it to everyone.

So I need 12 Bible Buddies.

That is the whole job. Install it, keep it on your phone for two weeks, use
it however you normally would. When the two weeks are up, Bible Buddy goes
live on the Play Store for everybody.

Two steps:

**Step 1 - join the testers**
https://groups.google.com/g/biblebuddy-android-testers
Tap "Join group". It will not email you.

**Step 2 - get the app**
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/net.mybiblebuddy.app
Tap "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play".

It has to be that order. If step 2 says you are not a tester, give step 1 a
minute to go through and try again.

It is the full app - the whole Bible with notes beside every chapter, the
daily reading plan, the audio, all of it. The same Bible Buddy you already
use, just on your home screen.

And if more than 12 of you come, even better. What counts is that 12 people
still have it installed at the end of the fortnight, so a few spare hands
helps.

Thank you. I cannot launch on Android without you.

Louis
