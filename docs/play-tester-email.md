# The Play Store tester email

Ready to send. Written 2026-11-03, the day Google approved the closed test.

## Why it is shaped like this

Google will not let an Android app go public until **12 testers have kept it
installed for 14 unbroken days**. Not 12 clicks - 12 people with it still on
their phone two weeks later. The email asks for 20 so the count survives the
people who install it and delete it on day three.

There are two steps and they cannot be merged, which is the awkward part:
joining the group is what gives someone permission to see the test, and the
second link is what actually installs it. Both links are below and both are
live. The group is set to "anyone on the web can join", so nobody has to be
approved and Louis does not have to collect a single email address.

Send to the whole list. Twenty out of several thousand is a low bar, and the
people who say yes are the ones who were going to open the app anyway.

## The email

Bible Buddy is on Android.

Well - almost. Google has approved it, but before they will put it in the
Play Store for everyone, they want a small group of people to test it first.
That is where you come in.

We need **20 people** to install it and keep it on their phone for **two
weeks**. That is the whole job. Use it like normal, or do not use it at all -
it just has to stay installed. After that, Bible Buddy goes live on the Play
Store for everyone.

Here is how to help. It is two taps:

**Step 1 - join the testers**
https://groups.google.com/g/biblebuddy-android-testers
Click "Join group". You will not get emails from it.

**Step 2 - get the app**
https://play.google.com/apps/testing/net.mybiblebuddy.app
Click "Become a tester", then "Download it on Google Play".

It has to be in that order, or Google will not show you the app. If step 2
says you are not a tester, give step 1 a minute to go through and try again.

Everything is there - the whole Bible with notes beside every chapter, the
daily reading plan, the audio, all of it. Same as the website, just on your
home screen.

Thank you. Genuinely - we cannot launch without you.

Louis
