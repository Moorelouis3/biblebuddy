# Bible Clue: card art to make

Generated by `npx tsx scripts/clue-art-needed.ts`. Every answer in the Genesis question bank that has no card picture yet.

**499 pictures to make** (each as a pair: a mystery silhouette and a revealed picture), plus **50 chapter cards** for the answers that are phrases, not things.

Same style as the existing deck cards (Joseph, Noah, Beersheba): the mystery one is the dark silhouette, the reveal one is the full-colour picture.

File names: `<file>-mystery.webp` and `<file>-reveal.webp` (or .png; I convert them), into public/games/clue/genesis/art/. A chapter card is `chapter-<NN>-mystery` / `chapter-<NN>-reveal`.
