# Bible Buddy Blog Post Format

Every Bible Buddy blog post follows this structure. Do not skip sections. Do not add sections that are not here.

## Voice rules

- Short sentences. Plain language. Direct teaching tone.
- No hyphens anywhere in the text.
- No academic or overly polished phrasing. Write like Louis talks when he teaches.
- Talk to one reader. Say "you," not "believers" or "people."
- Ground everything in Scripture. This is Bible teaching, not self help with verses sprinkled on.

## Length

- Standard posts: 3,000 to 3,500 words.
- Pillar topics (anxiety, fear, forgiveness, prayer, purpose): 4,500 to 5,000 words.
- Never pad. Every section must earn its length. If a section is thin, cut it, do not stretch it.

## Structure (in this exact order)

### 1. Title
SEO friendly. Main keyword in the title. Speaks to a real struggle or question. Example: "What Does the Bible Say About Anxiety? A Complete Guide for Christians."

### 2. Intro (5 to 6 short paragraphs, 300 to 400 words)
Meet the reader where they are. Describe what they are feeling in the first two paragraphs so they think "that is me." Then tell them what this post will give them. Main keyword appears in the first 100 words.

### 3. Why This Matters for Your Faith (200 to 300 words)
Why this topic matters to a Christian specifically. What is at stake spiritually. Why the world's answer is not enough.

### 4. The Core Teaching (1,200 to 1,800 words)
The main body. Break it into 4 to 7 numbered H3 sections, like steps. Each section is one clear idea with Scripture backing it. This is where the real teaching lives.

### 5. Practical Tips (300 to 400 words)
Numbered list, 6 to 8 tips. Each tip starts with a bolded short sentence, then 2 to 3 sentences explaining it. These are things the reader can do today.

### 6. Top 3 to 5 Bible Verses About [Topic] (500 to 800 words)
Each verse gets its own H3. Quote the verse, then break it down in 3 to 5 sentences. Not a full study, just enough to help them start with that verse. Use the same Bible translation the app uses.

### 7. Frequently Asked Questions (500 to 700 words)
8 to 10 questions as H3s. Use the exact questions people actually type into Google about this topic. Answer each in 3 to 5 sentences. Example questions for anxiety: Is anxiety a sin? Is God punishing me with anxiety? Can Christians take medication for anxiety?

### 8. Conclusion (150 to 250 words)
Recap the 2 or 3 most important takeaways. End with hope and one clear next action.

### 9. Join Bible Buddy CTA
Standard block at the end of every post. Invite the reader to join Bible Buddy to keep growing: devotionals, the community, the reading streak. One button or link to sign up. Warm, direct, no pressure.

## SEO checklist for every post

- Main keyword in title, first 100 words, and at least 2 H2 headings.
- Meta description under 160 characters.
- Internal links to 2 or 3 other Bible Buddy blog posts inside the body.
- FAQ questions phrased exactly how people search them.
