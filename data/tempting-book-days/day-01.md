Somebody has been studying you.

Not a person. Not a company tracking what you click.

Something older, and far more dangerous.

The Bible calls him the devil. And he has watched human beings long enough to know exactly where we bend.

Most people never think about that.

They treat temptation like weather. It just happens. A bad day, a weak moment, an urge that comes out of nowhere.

So they fight it blind, lose the same fight for years, and quietly wonder what is wrong with them.

**Here is what almost nobody sees.**

Temptation is not random. It runs on a system.

The devil has used the same three tricks since the first garden on earth, and he is still running them on you this week.

This study is a twenty one day walk through the greatest fight in history: the day that same devil met Jesus in the wilderness and threw everything he had at Him.

Jesus saw every trick coming. He beat every single one. And He showed us how.

But before we watch that fight, we do what every good fighter does first.

**We study the opponent.**

# Meet the Opponent

When he walks up to Jesus in the wilderness, Matthew gives him a job title. He calls him "the tempter" (Matthew 4:3).

His other name tells you even more.

The name *Satan* means adversary. An adversary is an opponent. The one standing across from you in a fight.

That is what he is to you. Not a myth. Not a mood. An opponent with a plan.

And his plan is simple.

**God wants everything good for you:**

* 🌱 To grow
* ✨ To be holy
* 🎯 To live with purpose
* 👣 To look more like Jesus every year

The adversary wants the exact opposite. He wants you stuck in cycles of sin, shame, and hiding, until you are too worn down to be a threat to him.

## His Number One Weapon

His number one weapon for that job is temptation.

Temptation is an invitation to sin. It is the enemy luring you to live life on your own terms, by your own rules, with your back turned to God.

Now here is the part that should change how you fight.

**He is not creative.**

He has been running the same three tricks since the beginning of time, and the Bible hands us the whole list in one verse:

> "For all that is in the world, the lust of the flesh, and the lust of the eyes, and the pride of life, is not of the Father, but is of the world." (1 John 2:16)

*Lust* is an old word for a craving so strong it starts giving orders.

Keep that in mind, because every temptation you will ever face lands in one of these three buckets.

## The Three Tricks

**🔥 The lust of the flesh** is the craving of the body.

It is the part of you that says, *I want it right now.* The video you know you should not watch. The bottle. The plate. The temper you let off its leash because it feels good for ten seconds.

The flesh does not ask if it is right. It demands to be fed, instantly, no matter what God says.

**👁️ The lust of the eyes** is the craving for what you see.

This one is greed, envy, and comparison. You see somebody else's money, house, body, marriage, or following, and the wanting starts to eat you alive.

It is reaching for more, not because you need it, but because you saw it.

**👑 The pride of life** is the sneakiest of the three, because it does not look dirty. It looks confident.

It is the urge to run your own show. To be your own god. To force life onto your timeline, call your own shots, and test God instead of trusting Him.

Pride says, *I should be the one deciding here.*

The body. The eyes. The ego.

**Three doors into one heart.** Every attack he has ever launched comes through one of them.

## Watch the Playbook Get Written

Do you want proof? Walk back to the very first temptation on earth.

> "Now the serpent was more subtil than any beast of the field which the LORD God had made. And he said unto the woman, Yea, hath God said, Ye shall not eat of every tree of the garden?" (Genesis 3:1)

*Subtil* is an old word for sneaky and crafty.

And look at his opening move. He does not command Eve to sin. He asks her a question.

"Yea, hath God said?" Did God really say that? Are you sure?

Temptation almost always starts there, by blurring what God said until you are not sure you heard Him right.

First he fogs the Word. Then he makes his offer.

Now watch what Eve sees in the seconds before she falls:

> "And when the woman saw that the tree was good for food, and that it was pleasant to the eyes, and a tree to be desired to make one wise, she took of the fruit thereof, and did eat." (Genesis 3:6)

Read that list slowly.

* 🔥 **Good for food.** The lust of the flesh.
* 👁️ **Pleasant to the eyes.** The lust of the eyes.
* 👑 **Desired to make one wise.** The pride of life.

There it is.

The same three tricks John named, already running on page three of the Bible. The devil opened history with this playbook, and he has never needed a new one.

Why would he? It keeps working.

It took out Adam and Eve in a perfect garden. It tripped Israel in the wilderness over and over. It brought down David, a man God Himself called a man after His own heart, with one look from one rooftop.

## They Only Work in the Dark

But the tricks have a weakness, and it matters for everything ahead.

**They only work in the dark.**

A trick that has been explained is halfway beaten.

As long as temptation feels random, you stay a victim of the weather. The moment you can stand in the middle of a craving and name it — *this is the lust of the flesh, this is the eyes, this is my pride* — the disguise falls off.

You are no longer being swept away by a mystery. You are looking at an opponent, and you know his move.

That is why this study starts here, with the enemy's playbook open on the table.

## What To Watch For In Today's Reading

* 📜 The three things John says are in the world
* 🐍 The serpent's first words to the woman — notice he opens with a question, not an order
* 🍎 What the tree looked like to Eve, and how many things she saw
* ⏳ What passes away, and who abides for ever

You don't have to answer all of those right now. Just notice them as you read.

## The Bigger Takeaway

One Man stood in a wilderness, starving and alone, while the tempter ran all three tricks on Him at full strength.

The flesh. The eyes. The pride. One after another.

**And He did not bend once.**

For the next twenty days we are going to watch Him do it, move by move, and learn to fight the way He fought.

But the first move belongs to you, today.

The devil has only ever had three tricks, and now you know their names. One of them is his favorite door into your life. Before this day ends, we are going to find it.

## Today's One Thing

**Name the Trick**

The devil has used the same three tricks since Eden. They only work in the dark. Today you turn the light on.

1. Write the three tricks on a piece of paper: the flesh, the eyes, and pride.
2. Circle the one that owns the most ground in your life.
3. Under the circle, write one honest sentence naming your version of it: *"For me it looks like ______."*

Not a confession for anyone else to read. One true sentence between you and God.

Write it, fold it, and keep it. This study is going to hand you weapons to write next to it.
