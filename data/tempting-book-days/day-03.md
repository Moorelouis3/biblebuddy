Somewhere along the way, somebody sold us a lie.

The lie goes like this: once you decide to follow God, life smooths out. Temptation fades. The pull gets weaker. Doing the right thing starts to feel natural, because now you are on God's team and He is on yours.

Then you actually start following Him, and the opposite happens.

The pull gets stronger. The fight gets louder. Things that never bothered you before suddenly will not leave you alone.

**If that has happened to you, you are not broken. You are not doing it wrong.**

Something else is going on, and Jesus told us about it to our face, in two of the most sobering verses He ever spoke.

Just two verses. But inside them is the honest answer to a question every believer eventually asks in the dark: *why is this so hard?*

Jesus never promised easy. He promised something better.

# The Narrow Road

Think back to the wilderness for a second.

Jesus was led in there to face the devil head on, and nothing about it was comfortable. It was not a retreat. It was not a quiet getaway with God.

It was war. He was hungry, tired, and isolated, with Satan himself pushing on Him.

**That is the setup for real temptation.**

It does not usually come when you are rested and surrounded by friends. It comes when you are weak, when you are alone, when you are desperate.

A lot of people think that once they decide to follow God, temptation should just back off. Like life is supposed to get easier because they are finally doing the right thing.

That has never been how it works. It is actually the opposite.

## Why the Heat Turns Up

The second you start taking God seriously, really trying to walk the narrow path, the enemy turns up the heat.

Why would he waste ammunition on somebody drifting away from God on their own?

He saves his fire for the ones who are becoming dangerous. A child of God who actually lives sold out is a threat to everything he is building.

Jesus warned us this walk would cost something. Here is how He said it:

> "Enter ye in at the strait gate: for wide is the gate, and broad is the way, that leadeth to destruction, and many there be which go in thereat: Because strait is the gate, and narrow is the way, which leadeth unto life, and few there be that find it." (Matthew 7:13–14)

That word *strait* is an old word, and it does not mean straight like a line.

It means narrow. Tight. A gate you have to turn sideways and squeeze through, carrying nothing extra.

Picture the scene when He said it. Jesus is standing on a hillside, crowds everywhere, finishing the greatest sermon ever preached. He has been teaching about anger, lust, loving enemies, giving, prayer, fasting, trusting God instead of money.

The whole blueprint for living under God's rule.

Then He looks at them and tells the truth: *look, this is not the easy way. Most people will not take it.*

That is not a verse meant to scare you. It is a verse meant to wake you up.

## Why the Broad Road Is Broad

Think about why the broad road is broad.

The devil is not setting up obstacles on it. He does not have to.

**That road is already slicked downhill with everything your flesh wants, everything your eyes crave, everything your pride is dying for.**

It is comfortable. It is popular. Everybody is on it.

But the narrow road? That is different.

* 🪨 That is sacrifice
* 🛑 That is self control
* 🗣️ That is telling your flesh no when it is screaming yes
* 🎣 That is resisting the bait when the devil dangles it right in front of your face

And that is exactly why he works so hard to knock you off it.

Peter, a man who knew a thing or two about being targeted, put it as bluntly as it can be put:

> "Be sober, be vigilant; because your adversary the devil, as a roaring lion, walketh about, seeking whom he may devour:" (1 Peter 5:8)

Be sober, be vigilant. Stay clear headed. Stay awake at your post.

Because your adversary, that same opponent from Day 1, is not strolling around aimlessly. He is hunting.

A lion does not chase the whole herd. He watches for the one who wandered off alone, the one who stopped paying attention, the one who fell asleep in the open.

## Being Hunted Means You Are Worth Hunting

Here is the part most people never flip around.

If following Jesus has made your life harder in some ways, if the fight has gotten louder since you got serious, that is not evidence that you took a wrong turn.

That is evidence that you left the broad road, where the enemy never had to lift a finger against you, and stepped onto the one road he actually guards.

So why take the hard road at all?

**Because of where it goes.**

Look at the end of each verse again. The broad way leads to destruction. The narrow way "leadeth unto life."

Real life. True joy, the kind that cannot be taken away from you. Eternity with God.

The devil knows what is waiting at the end of that road. That is why he fights you for every step of it.

## What To Watch For In Matthew 7

* 🚪 What the wide gate and broad way are like
* ⚠️ Where the broad way leads
* 🌿 What the narrow way leads to
* 🔢 How few find it — the numbers are not what we would hope

You don't have to answer all of those right now. Just notice them as you read.

## The Bigger Takeaway

Let this settle into you today.

**Hard does not mean you are failing.**

Hard is what the right road feels like under the feet of somebody the enemy takes seriously.

You were never promised a road without resistance. You were promised a road that ends in life.

The only thing left to figure out is where the broad road pulls at you the hardest.

## Today's One Thing

**One Narrow Choice**

The narrow road is not walked in theory. It is walked one choice at a time.

1. Name the one place in your week where the broad road pulls hardest.
2. Make one narrow road choice there today, on purpose.
3. Tonight, write down what you chose and how it felt afterward.

One choice will not win the war. But it tells your flesh who is in charge, and it tells God you meant what you prayed.

## Today's Prayer

Father,

Thank You for telling me the truth instead of selling me a fantasy. You never promised easy. You promised life.

I confess the broad road looks good to me some days. It is comfortable. It is crowded. My flesh loves everything about it.

But I know where it ends, and I do not want to end there.

Give me strength for the narrow road when my flesh is screaming for the wide one.

Keep me sober and awake, because I know the lion is watching for the moment I drift.

And when the fight gets loud, remind me what it means: I am on the road worth guarding, headed to the life You promised.

In Jesus' name, amen.
