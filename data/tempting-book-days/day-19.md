At the very beginning of this study, I made you a promise.

I said that by the end, you would have the five steps to fighting temptation like Jesus did.

Not five tips for trying harder. Not a pep talk. **The actual blueprint**, pulled straight out of the story we have spent eighteen days walking through.

Today I keep that promise.

Here is the thing: you have already seen every one of these steps. You watched them happen.

They were in the temple when He was twelve, in the hidden years, at the river, in the fasting, and in all three swings of the sword.

Today we pull them out of the story and lay them in your hand, numbered and ready.

So today we read the whole wilderness account one more time, start to finish. But read it differently this time.

**Read it like a fighter studying film of a champion.**

Because that is exactly what it is. And starting today, it is your film too.

# The Blueprint

Look back at the whole war for a moment.

Three swings: the stones, the kingdoms, the pinnacle. The flesh, the eyes, the pride.

Three times the tempter brought his best, against a Man at His physically weakest, and three times the sword came out: *it is written.*

Then the devil left, out of ammunition, and Jesus walked out of that desert carrying more power than He carried in.

Nobody wins a fight like that by accident. Underneath those three answers was a whole way of living, built long before the desert.

**That is the blueprint.** Five steps, every one of them pulled from the story you now know by heart.

## Step One: Know Whose You Are Before the Fight

Before the first temptation ever came, the question of identity was already settled.

At twelve, Jesus knew He had to be about His Father's business. At the river, the Father declared it out loud: *this is My beloved Son.*

So when the devil opened with *if thou be the Son of God*, he was swinging at solid rock.

That is where your fight starts too, and it is why I had you write the sentence on Day 5: **I belong to God.**

The devil's first move is almost always aimed at identity, because a person who does not know whose they are will take almost any offer.

A person who does, fights *from* approval instead of *for* it.

## Step Two: Prepare in the Quiet

Eighteen hidden years in Nazareth. Forty days of fasting and prayer. The Word stored so deep it could be drawn blindfolded.

Jesus did not cram for the desert. **He walked in already full.**

You cannot cram for an ambush. The preparing happens now, in the unglamorous daily things: the morning manna, the hidden habit, the verse cards.

Quiet seasons are not the off season. They are training camp.

## Step Three: See the Attack for What It Is

Jesus never once mistook the offer for friendship. Bread, kingdoms, pinnacle: He saw each one instantly as the trick it was.

You have the same scouting report now:

* 🔥👁️👑 **Three tricks:** the flesh, the eyes, the pride
* 🪜 **Five steps down:** the thought, the desire, the justification, the action, the pattern

**Temptation named is temptation half beaten.**

The moment you can stand inside a craving and say, *this is the lust of the flesh working my staircase*, the disguise is off and the fog is gone.

## Step Four: Answer Out Loud With What Is Written

Not arguments. Not willpower. Not negotiating with the whisper in your own head.

Jesus spoke the written Word into the air, three for three, and the conversation kept ending.

The sword works in your hand exactly like it worked in His, but only if it was stored before the fight and only if it actually comes out.

Say the verse. Out loud. **The meeting breaks up every time.**

## Step Five: Trust the Father's Timing and Take His Way Out

Jesus stayed hungry rather than eat outside His Father's plan. He refused the crown that skipped the cross. He would not jump and force heaven's hand.

Underneath all three refusals was one settled trust: *My Father decides, and My Father provides.*

That trust has a promise attached to it for you, and it is so important that it gets all of Day 21 to itself: **God always builds a way of escape.**

Your job in the moment is to trust His timing and take His exit, even when the exit costs your pride.

## What Is Not on the List

Read the blueprint again, and notice what is missing: **be perfect.**

The blueprint is not a performance you grade yourself on. It is a practice you grow into, and the same Spirit who led Jesus in and filled Him on the way out is the one leading you.

Some days you will run all five steps and win clean. Some days you will catch yourself at step three with the staircase already creaking.

Run the steps from wherever you are standing. **They work mid fall too.**

## What To Watch For In Luke 4

* 🪨 What was settled about Jesus before the first attack came
* 📖 What He had stored up that the desert could not take
* 👁️ How fast He recognizes each trick for what it is
* 🔄 Verse 1 and verse 14 together: He goes in full of the Spirit and comes out in the power of the Spirit

You don't have to answer all of those right now. Just notice them as you read.

## The Bigger Takeaway

Remember how the film ends: *he departed for a season, and Jesus returned in the power of the Spirit.*

**Both halves are yours now.**

The enemy will be back, and you can come out of every round stronger than you went in.

## Today's One Thing

**The Card**

A blueprint on a shelf builds nothing. Yours goes on the wall today.

1. Write the five steps on one card, in your own words, short enough to read in ten seconds.
2. Put the card where your biggest temptation actually happens: the kitchen, the desk, next to the phone charger.
3. Before you sleep tonight, confirm it is up. That is the whole assignment.

Jesus walked into the desert carrying everything He needed. This card is you, packing.

## Today's Prayer

Father,

Thank You for not just winning the fight, but filming it for me. Every move Your Son made in that desert was made in front of witnesses, so people like me could learn it.

You know my weak step. You have watched the enemy aim at it for years.

Walk me through Your Son's steps until they become mine.

Settle my identity before the fight. Train me in the quiet. Sharpen my eyes to see the attack, my tongue to speak Your Word, and my heart to trust Your timing and take Your way out.
