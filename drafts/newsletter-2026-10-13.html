One Chapter a Day Through Proverbs

Hey {{first_name}}

Proverbs is thirty-one chapters long. Most months are thirty-one days long. That is not a coincidence anybody planned, but it does make the book unusually easy to live with: one chapter, once a day, and in a month you have read the whole thing.

It is a strange book to read straight through. It does not tell a story. It rarely explains itself. A verse about a lazy man sits next to a verse about a king, and neither one stops to say why. Read quickly and it feels like a list. Read slowly, one chapter at a time, and it starts to sound like somebody who has watched people for a very long while and is telling you what he noticed.

The Wisdom of Proverbs walks through all thirty-one chapters, one day at a time, in plain English. Each day takes a few minutes. If you miss a day, the next one is still there — the study waits for you rather than the other way round.

You do not need to start on the first of the month. Day 1 is ready whenever you are.
