ONE CHAPTER A DAY THROUGH PROVERBS

Hey Daniel

Proverbs is thirty-one chapters long. Most months are thirty-one days
long. That is not a coincidence anybody planned, but it does make the
book unusually easy to live with: one chapter, once a day, and in a
month you have read the whole thing.

It is a strange book to read straight through. It does not tell a
story. It rarely explains itself. A verse about a lazy man sits next to
a verse about a king, and neither one stops to say why. Read quickly
and it feels like a list. Read slowly, one chapter at a time, and it
starts to sound like somebody who has watched people for a very long
while and is telling you what he noticed.

The Wisdom of Proverbs walks through all thirty-one chapters, one day
at a time, in plain English. Each day takes a few minutes. If you miss
a day, the next one is still there - the study waits for you rather
than the other way round.

You do not need to start on the first of the month. Day 1 is ready
whenever you are.

WHAT GOD'S NAMES ACTUALLY MEAN

Yahweh. El Shaddai. Adonai. They get translated into English as "LORD"
and "God" and "Lord", which flattens them into near enough the same
word - and hides the fact that the original writers were reaching for
different things each time.

This one goes through the main names of God in Scripture and what each
actually carries: where it appears, who used it, and what it meant to
the person saying it.

HAVE THEY FOUND NOAH'S ARK?

There is a boat-shaped formation at Durupinar in eastern Turkey, and
every few years it is back in the headlines as the Ark. Scans have been
run on it. Teams have published findings. The claims are confident.

We went through what the research actually says - what has been
measured, what geologists make of the formation, and what is still an
