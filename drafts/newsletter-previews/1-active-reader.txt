WHY PROVERBS READS DIFFERENTLY

It doesn't tell a story.

It rarely explains itself.

A verse about a lazy man sits right next to a verse about a king.
Neither one stops to say why.

So read it quickly and it feels like a list.

Read it slowly, one chapter at a time, and it starts to sound like
someone who has watched people for a very long time telling you what
he noticed.

WHAT GOD'S NAMES ACTUALLY MEAN

Yahweh. El Shaddai. Adonai.

In English they all come out as "LORD" or "God".

So they end up sounding like the same word - and you lose the fact
that the writers were reaching for different things each time.

We went through the main ones:

- Where each name appears
- Who used it, and when
- What it meant to the person saying it

Read: https://www.mybiblebuddy.net/blog/names-of-god-meanings

HAVE THEY FOUND NOAH'S ARK?

There's a boat-shaped formation at Durupinar in eastern Turkey.

Every few years it's back in the headlines as the Ark.

Scans have been run. Teams have published findings. The claims are
confident.

So we went through what the research actually says:

- What has genuinely been measured
- What geologists make of the formation
- What's still an open question, not a settled one
