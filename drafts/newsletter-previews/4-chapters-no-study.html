Why Proverbs Reads Differently

It doesn't tell a story.

It rarely explains itself.

A verse about a lazy man sits right next to a verse about a king. Neither one stops to say why.

So read it quickly and it feels like a list.

Read it slowly, one chapter at a time, and it starts to sound like someone who has watched people for a very long time telling you what he noticed.
